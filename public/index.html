The workshop

How a die gets made here.

  1. I

    Sculpt

    Every marked set starts as a master we sculpt ourselves. No stock molds with a logo slapped on.

  2. II

    Pour

    Small batches of resin, pigment and powder. No two come out the same, which is half the fun.

  3. III

    Ink

    Numbers are inked by hand, one face at a time.

  4. IV

    Check

    Each die is balance-checked before it ships. Mike and his wife make, inspect and pack every order together.

Mike has a BFA from Mizzou and spent a decade in a sign shop running large-format printers. The ogre handles quality control. He is very proud of his work. His mom is too.
