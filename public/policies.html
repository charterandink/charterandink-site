The fine print

Shipping, returns and privacy

Shipping

Orders ship from Jefferson City, Missouri, within 3 to 5 business days. You'll get a tracking number by email when your order ships.

Shipping is a flat $7 per order within the United States. Orders over $75 ship free.

Returns

Every die is poured and finished by hand, so small bubbles, swirls and variations are part of the piece, not defects. If something arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, email us within 14 days with a photo and we'll make it right with a replacement or refund.

Privacy

Payments are handled by Square. We never see or store your card number. We use your name, address and email only to fill and ship your order. If you join our email list, we'll only use it to tell you about new dice, and you can ask to be removed anytime.

Questions

Email mike@charterandink.com. A real person answers, usually the same day.
