The Workshop

Where the dice come from.

Every die starts as a sculpture on this bench. Here's how they're made, and some of what's come off it lately.

How it's made

  1. I

    Sculpt

    Every sigil starts as a master sculpted by hand. No stock molds with a logo slapped on.

  2. II

    Pour

    Small batches of resin, pigment and powder. No two come out the same.

  3. III

    Ink

    Numbers inked by hand, one face at a time.

  4. IV

    Check

    Every die is balance-checked before it ships.

The makers

Charter & Ink is Mike and his wife. Mike has a BFA from Mizzou and has been making art for more than 25 years, from digital to 3D. Together they sculpt, pour, inspect and pack every order.

The studio also employs one ogre. Steelfrenzy handles quality control. He is very proud of his work. His mom is too.
