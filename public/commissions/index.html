Commissions

Got an idea? We'd love to hear it.

A die for your character's sigil. A set that matches your campaign's world. A chonk for the friend who rolls nothing but ones. If you can describe it, there's a good chance we can sculpt it.

We take on a small number of commissions at a time, so every piece gets the same hands-on attention as our own work. When the spots are full, we'll let you know when the next one opens.

What we make

  • Custom chonks, 35mm or 55mm, with your own sigil or colorway
  • Sharp-edge sets built around a character, class or campaign
  • Gifts for your DM, your party, or the player who has everything

Where pricing starts

Custom chonks from $100

Custom sets from $150

Final price depends on the sculpting, the pour and the finish. You'll get an exact quote before anything is made.

How it works

  1. 1

    Tell us your idea

    Fill out the form below. Rough is fine. Reference images help.

  2. 2

    We sketch and quote

    We'll reply with a concept and a price, usually within a few days.

  3. 3

    50% deposit

    That holds your spot and starts the sculpting.

  4. 4

    We sculpt, pour and finish

    We'll send progress photos along the way.

  5. 5

    Balance, then it ships

    Most commissions take 1 to 2 months from deposit to doorstep.

Need it by a certain date? Tell us in the form. If we can't make your deadline, we'll say so upfront.

A few limits: we make original designs only, so no licensed characters, logos or trademarks. We're happy to make something in the spirit of a thing you love, just not a copy of it.
